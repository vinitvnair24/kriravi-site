Refunds & cancellation

Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy covers purchases of Kriravi Tech Labs products, such as the one-time unlock in Clock Learning.

Digital products sold through Google Play

  • Within 48 hours of purchase, you can request a refund directly from Google Play (play.google.com → Account → Order history → Request a refund).
  • After 48 hours and up to 7 days, email us at vinit.v.nair@kriravi.com with your Google Play order number (starting GPA.). If the purchase was accidental, or the feature does not work as described and we can't fix it, we will refund the full amount.
  • Approved refunds are issued through Google Play to your original payment method, usually within 5–7 working days.

Cancellation

Our purchases are one-time and not subscriptions, so there is nothing recurring to cancel. A purchase is delivered instantly, so it cannot be cancelled once completed; please request a refund instead.

Accidental purchases by children

Our children's apps place a grown-up check before every purchase. If a child still makes a purchase by mistake, contact us and we will refund it.

Restoring a purchase

If you reinstall or change devices, sign in with the same Google account and use “Restore purchase” in the app. You will not be charged again.
