Clock Learning — privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Clock Learning (package com.vinit.clocklearning) is published by Kriravi Tech Labs, a sole proprietorship of Vinit Vijaykumar Nair. It is made for children aged 3 to 8 and their parents.

Short version

Clock Learning collects no personal data. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts.

Microphone

The “Say the time” game uses the microphone so your child can say the time aloud. Speech is recognised on the device itself. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. If speech recognition isn't available, the child taps an answer instead.

Notifications

The app can show a gentle daily practice reminder for the first 15 days. It is created on the device; no data leaves the device.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment; we receive only the order record Google provides. A grown-up check is shown before the Google Play payment screen.

Children's privacy

The app complies with Google Play's Families policy. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.
