About Kriravi Tech Labs

A small independent studio in India.

Kriravi Tech Labs is a sole proprietorship founded in 2026 by Vinit Vijaykumar Nair. We make practical products that fix real, everyday problems: mobile apps for families today, and electronic devices for industry next.

The name

KRIRAVI joins three names from the founder's family. It also echoes two Sanskrit words: kri, to make, and ravi, the sun — a maker of bright, useful things.

How we build

  • Privacy first. Our children's apps have no ads, no analytics and no accounts.
  • Honest pricing. Free to try, then a single one-time purchase. No subscriptions in children's apps.
  • Grounded in industry. Our device work draws on more than a decade in plastics and injection moulding.
