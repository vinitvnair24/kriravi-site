Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy explains how Kriravi Tech Labs (“we”), a sole proprietorship of Vinit Vijaykumar Nair, handles personal data for this website and our apps. Each app also has its own short policy, linked from Apps & pricing.

What we collect

  • Website: this site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security.
  • Email: if you write to us, we keep your message and email address to reply.
  • Apps: our apps collect no personal data unless the app's own policy says otherwise.
  • Purchases: payments are handled by Google Play. We receive an order record from Google (order number, product, amount, country) but never your card or bank details.

How we use it

Only to answer you, provide what you bought, meet legal and tax duties, and prevent fraud. We do not sell or rent personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Children

Our children's apps are designed for Google Play's Families policy. They contain no ads and collect no personal information from children.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run the business (Google Play, our email and hosting providers), or when the law requires it.

Keeping and protecting data

We keep emails and order records only as long as needed for support and legal requirements (typically up to 8 years for tax records), and protect them with reasonable security measures.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Write to vinit.v.nair@kriravi.com; we respond within 30 days.
